# Google Ads for anodeh.com — South Africa only

Advertise AnodeH as **Google Ads click protection + spam email monitoring** to South African buyers.

## Campaign goal

Prefer **Conversions** (Sales / Subscribe), not Traffic alone.

1. In Google Ads → **Goals** / **Conversions**, confirm the AnodeH purchase conversion is recording (thank-you / PayPal success).
2. Create a campaign with objective **Sales** / **Website conversions** (or Search with conversion bidding).
3. Conversion action: the existing AnodeH purchase / subscribe event on `anodeh.com`.

If conversion volume is still low, start with **Maximize clicks** or **Maximize conversions** with a low target CPA, then switch fully to conversions after ~30 purchases.

## Location: South Africa only

1. Campaign settings → **Locations**
2. Choose **South Africa**
3. Presence: **Presence: People in or regularly in your targeted locations** (not “interested in”)
4. Remove any other countries / radius targets

## Networks and language

- Search Network: on
- Display Network: off for the first SA Search campaign
- Languages: **English** (add Afrikaans later if you expand)

## Destination

- Final URL: `https://anodeh.com`
- Prefer landing on `/` or `/register?plan=month` (1 website free trial)
- Optional: `/#spam` when the search is about inbox / phishing
